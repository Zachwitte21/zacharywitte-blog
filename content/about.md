I'm Zachary Witte. I build AI agents, MCP servers, and cloud systems, and I write about what I learn along the way on this blog.

## What I do

**By day**, I work at DCS Corporation on AI-focused projects. Roughly half of that work is in the CBRN (chemical, biological, radiological, and nuclear) space, and the other half is on a mix of other AI projects.

**In the evenings and on weekends**, I'm building technology to improve quality of life for people living with dementia and their caregivers. That work is the Sollund Dementia Wellness mobile app, and you can learn more at [sollundhealth.com](https://sollundhealth.com). It grew out of a reminiscence therapy agent I built for a Google hackathon in January, which I wrote about in [Takeaways From Google Hackathon](/posts/takeaways-from-google-hackathon/).

## What I work on and write about

- **Sollund and mobile app development**: building and launching an app for dementia wellness, including the business lessons I learned along the way ([Learning Business Lessons the Hard Way](/posts/planning-the-business-before-the-app/)).
- **AI agents**: designing, building, and evaluating agents. This is where I have the most experience, and I'm trying to share more of it here going forward ([AI Agents With MCP](/posts/ai-agents-with-mcp/), [Agent Evals With Inspect](/posts/agent-evals-with-inspect/)).
- **Cloud environments**: hands-on experience building and deploying on Google Cloud, AWS, and Azure.

You can see what I've built on the [Projects](/projects/) page, and what I've been thinking about lately on the [Blog](/posts/).

## Background

I studied at the University of Delaware, where I was part of a six-student drone research team. We scanned buildings with drones and used photogrammetry and machine learning to turn the scans into 3D models, which I then used to build a virtual tour of the campus. That project, along with my other coursework, is where I built my foundational understanding of AI.
